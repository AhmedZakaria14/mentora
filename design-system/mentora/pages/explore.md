# Explore — Mentora Design Override

Inherits `design-system/mentora/MASTER.md`.

## Page goal
Move the learner from a vague problem/query to a confident shortlist of experts.

## Overrides
- Density: 7/10 — more information per viewport than homepage.
- Motion: 3/10 — results should feel stable while filtering.
- Search remains visible near top.
- Filters use compact controls and horizontal overflow on mobile.
- Result cards emphasize: relevance reason, expertise, nearest availability, session price, profile CTA.
- Match percentage is advisory/demo only; do not imply scientific certainty.
- Demo experts must remain explicitly marked as examples.
- Avoid fake reviews or fabricated social proof.
- 375px: one-column results, sticky/scrollable filter row, no horizontal page overflow.
