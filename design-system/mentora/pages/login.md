# Login — Mentora Design Override

Inherits `design-system/mentora/MASTER.md`.

## Page goal
Get a user authenticated with minimum distraction while preserving confidence and context.

## Overrides
- One dominant authentication method: Google OAuth.
- Never ask Calendar/Meet permissions during basic sign-in.
- Show a calm explanation of what sign-in enables.
- Error states should be human-readable and stay on Mentora.
- Secondary option is browsing experts without sign-in.
- Avoid fake security claims; use process facts only.
- Mobile: login card first or immediately visible, CTA full width, touch targets >=44px.
